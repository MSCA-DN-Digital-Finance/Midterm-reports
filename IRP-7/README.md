# IRP-7 midterm report

**Risk index for cryptos**

- **Doctoral candidate** · Stefan Gaman
- **Affiliation** · Poznań University of Economics and Business

Paper catalogue snapshot dated 24 November 2026. [MSCA research profile](https://msca-dn-digital-finance.github.io/irps/irp-7.html)

## Research interests

The project examines large losses and common risk factors across cryptocurrency markets. It aims to develop an index that helps investors, regulators and the public assess how risk propagates across crypto assets.

The MSCA profile provides project scope and has no separate candidate interests listed.
