# IRP-10 midterm report

## Research interests

The project tests ways to reduce the time and computing resources used by AI in finance. It considers the practical and economic effects of these methods and their implications for sustainable finance.

**Candidate interests** · Green AI in finance, Advanced Statistical Modeling, Risk Management.
