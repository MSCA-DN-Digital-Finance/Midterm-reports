## Research interests

The project develops green credit scoring for retail and business borrowers. It studies whether combining environmental factors with information about borrower networks can improve credit decisions and access to financing.

**Candidate interests** · Machine learning, systemic risk, credit risk, probability of default, climate risk, extreme events.
