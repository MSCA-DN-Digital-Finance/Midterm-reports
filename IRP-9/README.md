# IRP-9 midterm report

**Audience-dependent explanations**

- **Doctoral candidate** · Patricia Marcella Evite
- **Affiliation** · University of Naples Federico II

Paper catalogue snapshot dated 24 November 2026. [MSCA research profile](https://msca-dn-digital-finance.github.io/irps/irp-9.html)

## Research interests

The project studies how explanations of complex financial models can be tailored to the people who use them. It examines what different audiences need to interpret model behavior and use the results in decisions.

**Candidate interests** · Blockchain, AI, Social and Solidarity Economy, Sustainable Finance, Economic and Financial Literacy.

## Working papers

- **Developing a Scale of Public Explanation Needs for AI-Assisted Financial Applications**

  Patricia Marcella Evite · Maria Iannario. In progress.

  Uses item response theory and 503 survey responses to develop and evaluate a scale for what the public wants explained about AI-assisted financial decisions.

  [Linked draft](https://github.com/MSCA-DN-Digital-Finance/WP3_eXplainable_and_Fair_AI/blob/main/IRP9/Papers/Public_Survey___IRT_Method.pdf) · [MSCA catalogue](https://msca-dn-digital-finance.github.io/Publications/working-papers.html#working-paper/developing-a-scale-of-public-explanation-needs-for-ai-assisted-financial-applications)

- **Reciprocity Propensity Scores: An ML-based Framework for Non-monetary Economies**

  Patricia Marcella Evite · Doina Bucur · Owen Chaffard · Will Ruddick. In progress.

  Develops measures of reciprocity from transaction histories and compares models for predicting whether participants will reciprocate in a non-monetary exchange network.

  [Linked draft](https://github.com/MSCA-DN-Digital-Finance/WP3_eXplainable_and_Fair_AI/blob/main/IRP9/Papers/Sarafu_Reciprocity_Score_WIP_Proposal.pdf) · [MSCA catalogue](https://msca-dn-digital-finance.github.io/Publications/working-papers.html#working-paper/reciprocity-propensity-scores-an-ml-based-framework-for-non-monetary-economies)

- **“More than a SHAP chart”: A Ground-Up Evaluation Criteria for Financial Supervisors**

  Patricia Marcella Evite · Ekaterina Svetlova · Doina Bucur. In progress.

  Uses central-bank supervisors’ requirements to develop X-TREAM, a prototype for assessing commercial banks’ AI models.

  [MSCA catalogue](https://msca-dn-digital-finance.github.io/Publications/working-papers.html#working-paper/more-than-a-shap-chart-a-ground-up-evaluation-criteria-for-financial-supervisors)
