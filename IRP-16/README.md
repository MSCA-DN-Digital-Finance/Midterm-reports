## Working papers

- **Can Large Language Models Construct Risk-Appropriate and Efficient Portfolios?**

  Jens Reil · Mohamed Faid · Julius Kooistra. In progress · Catalogue year 2027.

  Compares portfolios proposed by large language models across investor risk profiles with portfolios on the efficient frontier.

  [MSCA catalogue](https://msca-dn-digital-finance.github.io/Publications/working-papers.html#working-paper/can-large-language-models-construct-risk-appropriate-and-efficient-portfolios)

- **Evaluating Explainable Artificial Intelligence Methods for Financial Time Series Forecasting**

  Jens Reil · Branka H. Misheva · Lucia T. Gomez · Adam Kurpisz · Laura Spierdijk. In progress · Catalogue year 2027.

  Uses simulated financial series to test the reliability of SHAP, LIME and Integrated Gradients under different volatility and dependence patterns.

  [MSCA catalogue](https://msca-dn-digital-finance.github.io/Publications/working-papers.html#working-paper/evaluating-explainable-artificial-intelligence-methods-for-financial-time-series-forecasting)

- **Applications of Interpretable and Explainable Machine Learning in Insurance Pricing**

  Jens Reil · Axel Pison · Marcos R. Machado (2026). Under review.

  Compares machine learning with actuarial benchmarks for motor claims frequency and severity, using explanation tools and practitioner interviews to assess practical adoption.

  [MSCA catalogue](https://msca-dn-digital-finance.github.io/Publications/working-papers.html#working-paper/applications-of-interpretable-and-explainable-machine-learning-in-insurance-pricing)

- **Resolving the Duplicate-Feature Paradox with ReSHAP: A Redundancy-Weighted Generalization of Shapley Attribution**

  Adam Kurpisz · Jens Reil · Lucia T. Gomez · Branka H. Misheva (2026). Under review.

  Proposes weighting Shapley attributions by feature redundancy so that duplicate or highly correlated inputs receive less inflated shares of model credit.

  [MSCA catalogue](https://msca-dn-digital-finance.github.io/Publications/working-papers.html#working-paper/resolving-the-duplicate-feature-paradox-with-reshap-a-redundancy-weighted-generalization-of-shapley-attribution)
