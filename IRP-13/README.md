# IRP-13 midterm report

**Predicting financial trends using text mining and NLP**

- **Doctoral candidate** · Fulvio Raddi
- **Affiliation** · Babeș-Bolyai University (BBU)

Paper catalogue snapshot dated 24 November 2026. [MSCA research profile](https://msca-dn-digital-finance.github.io/irps/irp-13.html)

## Research interests

The project uses text mining and natural language processing to study financial trends, risk and fraud. It explores how signals from reports, online discussions and survey responses can inform models of sentiment, behavior and compliance.

**Candidate interests** · Econometrics, pricing derivatives, optimization, forecasting, statistics, central banking.
