# IRP-1 midterm report

**Strengthening European financial service providers through applicable reinforcement learning**

- **Doctoral candidate** · Mathis Jander
- **Affiliation** · University of Twente

Source snapshot dated 19 November 2026. [MSCA research profile](https://msca-dn-digital-finance.github.io/irps/irp-1.html)

## Research interests

The project investigates how European financial institutions can put reinforcement learning into practice. It considers decisions involving risk, multiple objectives and rare market conditions, along with the infrastructure, interpretability and oversight needed for deployment.

**Candidate interests** · Reinforcement Learning, Machine Learning, Time Series, Explainable Artificial Intelligence.
