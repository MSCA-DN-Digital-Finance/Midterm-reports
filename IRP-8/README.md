# IRP-8 midterm report

**Detecting anomalies and dependence structures in high dimensional, high frequency financial data**

- **Doctoral candidate** · David Siang-Li Jheng
- **Affiliation** · Bucharest University of Economic Studies (ASE)

Source snapshot dated 19 November 2026. [MSCA research profile](https://msca-dn-digital-finance.github.io/irps/irp-8.html)

## Research interests

The project investigates anomalies and dependencies in high-dimensional, high-frequency financial observations. It uses research on investor sentiment and herding to inform models of volatility and how assets move together.

**Candidate interests** · High-frequency data analysis, anomaly detection, tail risk and dependence modeling, machine learning in finance, FinTech innovation, option pricing.
