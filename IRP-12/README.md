# IRP-12 midterm report

**Developing industry-ready automated trading systems to conduct EcoFin analysis using deep learning algorithms**

- **Doctoral candidate** · Rahul Tak
- **Affiliation** · Bucharest University of Economic Studies (ASE)

Source snapshot dated 19 November 2026. [MSCA research profile](https://msca-dn-digital-finance.github.io/irps/irp-12.html)

## Research interests

The project develops deep-learning approaches for automated trading in financial institutions. Its scope includes operational reliability, model explainability, responsible investment and the ethics of automated decisions.

**Candidate interests** · Complex Dynamic Systems, AI for time series, industrial application of ML, XAI, network analysis and ESG.
